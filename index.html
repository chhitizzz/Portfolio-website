About me

I'm a QA Engineer and Automation Specialist passionate about building quality into software from day one — not bolting it on at the end. Over 5+ years I've worked across fintech, SaaS, and e-commerce, partnering with engineering teams to shift quality left.

I believe great QA is invisible — it's the reason releases feel smooth, users trust the product, and developers can ship with confidence. My work spans manual testing strategy, automation frameworks, CI/CD integration, and performance engineering.

When I'm not writing test suites, I contribute to open-source testing tools and write about automation best practices.
